Stochastic Workforce Scheduling & Shift Optimization

I optimized employee shifts, skills, breaks, overtime, and staffing levels under uncertain workload.

What I did

I built a workforce scheduling model where each employee has availability, skill, regular hours, break rules, and overtime limits. Demand changes by day and shift, so one fixed plan is not enough.

The optimization assigns people to shifts while keeping required skill coverage. It can also use overtime when needed, but overtime has a cost. Understaffing has a larger penalty, so the model finds a practical balance.

The portfolio solver view uses a demo configuration with 85 employees, 42 weekly shift slots, 4 skill groups, and 15 workload scenarios. These numbers show the scale of the optimization interface.

How the model works

  1. Prepare demand and employee data in Python.
  2. Create binary assignment variables and overtime variables.
  3. Add coverage, availability, skill, break, and hours constraints.
  4. Solve the mixed-integer model in Gurobi.
  5. Review cost, overtime, coverage, and robustness across scenarios.
Positive result: The model gives a confident staffing plan with clear cost and coverage trade-offs. It is easy to update when demand or employee availability changes.
