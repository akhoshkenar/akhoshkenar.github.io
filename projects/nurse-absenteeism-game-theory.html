Game-Theoretic Nurse Absenteeism Control

I used game theory to model nurse absenteeism by considering nurse incentives, rational behavior, and hospital staffing needs.

What I did

I treated attendance as a strategic decision. A nurse compares the benefit and cost of attending versus being absent. The hospital also chooses incentives and staffing actions.

The model gives each side a utility or payoff. Nurse utility can include wage, bonus, effort, fatigue, penalty, and personal cost. Hospital utility can include staffing cost, shortage cost, service quality, and absenteeism risk.

I used rational best-response behavior and equilibrium analysis to study which policy can improve attendance without creating excessive cost.

How the model works

  1. Write payoff functions for nurses and the hospital.
  2. Model Attend and Absent as main nurse actions.
  3. Calculate best responses and equilibrium attendance probabilities.
  4. Test bonus, penalty, reserve-staff, and mixed policy settings.
  5. Compare staffing gap and policy cost.
Positive result: The model gives a strong link between human behavior and staffing optimization. It explains why an incentive works, not only whether it works.
