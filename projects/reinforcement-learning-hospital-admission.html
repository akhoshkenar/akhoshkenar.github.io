Reinforcement Learning for Hospital Admission Control

I used reinforcement learning to control patient admission in a private hospital while considering bed capacity, patient value, and future demand.

What I did

I modeled patient admission as a sequential decision problem. The main actions are ADMIT, WAIT, and REJECT. The state can include ward occupancy, ICU occupancy, patient class, expected stay, urgency, and future demand information.

The reward function balances service, hospital revenue, capacity use, delay, and rejection cost. The agent learns that taking every patient now may not be best if high-priority demand can arrive later.

I wrote the environment and training logic in Python and used deep reinforcement-learning style methods such as PPO or DQN. The result is a policy that changes its action when the hospital state changes.

How the model works

  1. Define state, action, transition, and reward.
  2. Build a hospital admission environment in Python.
  3. Train an RL policy across many episodes.
  4. Compare the learned policy with a simple baseline.
  5. Inspect occupancy, reward, service, and admission decisions.
Positive result: The model can learn a flexible admission policy instead of using one fixed threshold. This is useful when future patient demand is uncertain.
