Vensim System Dynamics for Bank Profit & Government Policy

I used system dynamics in Vensim to study how banking parameters and government policies can change bank profit over time.

What I did

I created causal feedback loops between deposits, loans, interest income, profit, capital, customer trust, liquidity, and loan defaults. I also added government and regulatory variables.

The model includes reinforcing loops and balancing loops. For example, higher trust can increase deposits, more deposits can support lending, and profitable lending can increase capital. At the same time, too much lending can increase default risk and reduce profit.

I then converted the causal diagram into stock-flow logic. Policy rate, reserve requirement, loan guarantee, capital support, default shock, and subsidy can be changed to test different scenarios.

How the model works

  1. Build the causal-loop diagram.
  2. Mark positive and negative feedback links.
  3. Identify reinforcing and balancing loops.
  4. Create stock-flow equations in Vensim.
  5. Run policy scenarios and compare long-run bank outcomes.
Positive result: The model explains why a policy can help in one period and create a different effect later. It is useful for informed policy discussion because feedback is shown clearly.
