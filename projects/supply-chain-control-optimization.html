Supply Chain Control & Optimization

I designed an efficient supply-chain network, priced different parts, and tested the system with simulation and stochastic models.

What I did

I designed the flow from suppliers to production, warehouses, distribution points, and customers. I checked where material should move, how much inventory should be kept, and where cost was created.

I also worked on pricing for different parts. The pricing logic used unit cost, demand, lead time, service need, and margin. This gave management a simple way to compare pricing choices beside inventory choices.

I used a deterministic base case first. Then I added uncertain demand, uncertain lead time, and supply changes. The stochastic model showed how a plan can behave when real operations are not perfect.

How the model works

  1. Build the supply-chain network and cost structure.
  2. Estimate demand, lead-time, inventory, and price inputs.
  3. Run inventory and pricing decisions together.
  4. Create stochastic scenarios and simulation runs.
  5. Compare total cost, service, stockout risk, and margin.
Positive result: The result was a strong end-to-end decision model. It connected pricing, inventory, logistics, and uncertainty in one clear view.
