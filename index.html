Models that lead to decisions.

Each project card plays like a GIF. Click a project to open its own page, watch the full loop, and read the model, methods, measures, and outputs in easy English.

How I work. CMAN Seeks simple answers underneath complex systems. I build tools that minimize waste and support informed decisions. I hold each assumption beside the data. Some models are deterministic; others are stochastic. I define terminal states and admissible decisions when needed. I try to eliminate weak options, find a maximum or minimum, and stay confident about the method while being careful about the truth in the data. Simply put, I want analysis to land in practice. I use real operational questions, and in light of this, each new model shows what can happen before people enter a real decision.
