Operating Room Staff Flow & Infection Hotspot Simulation

I analyzed staff movement in an operating room, estimated transition rates, built a Markov-chain simulation, and identified high-density footprint areas.

What I did

I first analyzed movement records in Excel and Python. I calculated how often staff moved from one operating-room zone to another and converted these rates into a transition probability matrix.

Each operating-room zone is a Markov state. Staff can enter a new state according to the transition probabilities. The simulation uses different staff roles, including surgeon, nurse, anesthesia staff, technician, and circulating staff.

I stored movement footprints on the OR floor. Repeated movement creates a density map. High-density zones are then highlighted as possible contamination or infection-risk areas.

How the model works

  1. Clean timestamp and movement data.
  2. Estimate state-to-state transition probabilities.
  3. Create OR zones and staff agents in AnyLogic.
  4. Simulate staff movement with the Markov model.
  5. Aggregate footprints and create a floor heatmap.
Positive result: The project connects data analysis with simulation and spatial risk analysis. It gives a simple picture of where movement is concentrated during surgery.
