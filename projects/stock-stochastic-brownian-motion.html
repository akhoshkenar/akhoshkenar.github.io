Stochastic Process & Brownian Motion for Stock Prices

I used stochastic processes and Brownian motion to model stock price data and infer drift, volatility, and other parameters.

What I did

I started from stock prices and converted them to returns and log returns. I then used Brownian motion and geometric Brownian motion to represent random price movement over time.

The central model uses dS = μSdt + σSdW. Here μ is drift and σ is volatility. I estimated these parameters from data and checked the uncertainty around the estimates.

I used Monte Carlo simulation to generate many possible future price paths. These paths do not say one future is the truth. They show a distribution of admissible outcomes under the model assumptions.

How the model works

  1. Prepare prices and log returns.
  2. Estimate drift and volatility.
  3. Simulate Brownian and GBM sample paths.
  4. Create Monte Carlo distributions.
  5. Use bootstrap or confidence intervals for parameter inference.
Positive result: This project combines stochastic mathematics with real financial data. It gives a clear way to study uncertainty rather than hiding it behind one forecast.
